# Figma AI Prompt – Professional Electrical & Electronics Engineering Portfolio Website

Design a premium, modern, responsive portfolio website for **Sanju Grehi**, an Electrical & Electronics Engineering student specializing in **Industrial Automation, PLC, SCADA, Control Systems, Artificial Intelligence, Renewable Energy, and Electrical Engineering**.

The design should feel like a portfolio for an engineer applying to companies such as Siemens, Schneider Electric, ABB, Rockwell Automation, Honeywell, Emerson, GE Vernova, Tesla Energy, or similar technology companies.

## Overall Style

* Minimal, elegant, modern
* Apple + Linear + Vercel inspired UI
* Large whitespace
* Premium SaaS aesthetics
* Professional engineering feel
* Avoid gaming, cyberpunk, neon, or flashy designs
* Focus on readability and information hierarchy
* Responsive for Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile
